# Delivery Performance: Summary & Recommendations
**Scope:** 95,964 delivered orders, Sep 2016 to Aug 2018 | **On-time rate:** 92.3% | **Average delivery time:** 12.2 days

## Key Findings
- **Regional hotspots:** Late rates are highest in AL, MA, PI (14.9% to 23.2%), against 5.7% in Sao Paulo.
- **Shipping lanes:** Worst lanes are SP → AL (25.7% late); SP → MA (19.9% late); SP → PI (17.0% late). Cross-state orders are 1.5x more likely to be late than same-state orders.
- **Where time is lost:** Seller handling averages 2.8 days and carrier transit 9.0 days, so carrier transit is the larger share.
- **Peak season:** On-time rate fell to 79.5% in 2018-03 and 85.1% in 2018-02.
- **Customer impact:** Orders 7+ days late average 1.70 stars vs 4.29 for on-time orders.

## Recommendations
**1. Reset delivery promises in high-delay regions.** Add a 8-day buffer to estimates for AL, MA, PI, where late orders arrive about 8 days late. *Metric: on-time rate by state.*

**2. Fix the worst shipping lanes.** Pilot a regional fulfilment hub or alternative carrier on SP → AL, then SP → MA and SP → PI. *Metric: lane late %, carrier transit days.*

**3. Tighten seller handoff and plan for peak season.** Sellers average 2.8 days to hand over. Set a 2-day target, flag sellers above it, and add capacity before 2018-03-type peaks. *Metric: seller handling days, monthly on-time %.*

## Expected Impact
Late orders score 1.69 stars lower than on-time orders. Cutting the late rate by 2 percentage points would lift the average review by about 0.034 stars (based on this gap).

*Source: Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset (Kaggle). The dataset has no carrier name, so carrier performance is proxied by seller handling time, carrier transit time and shipping lane.*
